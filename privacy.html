Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Story Clips is a personal desktop tool. It runs only on the developer's own computer.

  • Data accessed: basic TikTok profile info (open ID) and permission to upload videos, granted via TikTok Login.
  • Storage: access tokens are stored locally on the developer's computer only. No data is sent to any server other than TikTok's official API.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared or used for advertising or analytics.
  • Deletion: revoke access in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions); local tokens are then deleted.
